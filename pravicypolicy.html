3. Launcher and App Drawer Data

DramaHome may offer an optional Android home screen and launcher experience. If you choose to set DramaHome as your default Home app through Android system settings, your home screen layout may change. You can switch to another Home app through your device settings.

To provide launcher, app drawer, search, shortcut, and commonly used app features, DramaHome may process limited information about apps installed on your device, such as app names, package names, app icons, launchable activities, app categories, pinned apps, recent app interactions, and home screen layout preferences.

We use this information to show your app drawer, help you find and launch apps, personalize shortcuts, maintain launcher layout, and improve reliability. We do not use launcher access to read the private content inside other apps, such as your messages, files, photos, banking activity, or passwords.

If Android or Google Play requires a narrower package visibility method for a feature, we aim to use the least broad visibility that reasonably supports the launcher function. If broader app visibility is used, it is for launcher, app drawer, search, compatibility, and user-initiated app launch functions.
